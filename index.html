Questions

Good to know.

Anything not covered here, ask in the form or by email — we’d rather over-explain than over-promise.

Can I hire you for just video, or just editing?
Yes. Filming only, editing only, filming and editing together, full social media management, or targeted ads are all separate options. Pick one or combine them, and add more later.
What does the marketing layer include?
Targeted ad campaigns on social platforms, built around your audience, with a monthly metrics report covering reach, clicks, leads and cost per result. It can be added to any social media package or run on its own. Ad spend is paid to the platform and is separate from our fee.
How is pricing decided?
Every project is quoted to fit. We look at how much you need, who is filming, where, how fast you need it, and what’s included. Fill out the quote form and you’ll have a clear number within one business day.
What kinds of projects do you take on?
Commercials, real estate, event coverage, short films, documentaries, music videos, content for religious institutions and non-profits, and ongoing social content for businesses and creators.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. Monthly work is month-to-month, with 14 days’ notice to pause or cancel. One-off projects are scoped and agreed up front.
Where do you shoot, and can you come to me?
We have crews in Dallas–Fort Worth, Miami, Chicago, New York and Los Angeles, with no travel charge in those metros. Anywhere else, we fly out or direct a vetted local crew, and our editors handle post either way. Travel is quoted per project.
Who owns the footage and the final videos?
You do. You get full commercial rights to everything we produce for you, plus the raw files on request. We only ask permission to feature work in our portfolio.
How do payments work?
Monthly clients are billed on the same date each month. One-off projects are 50% to book and 50% on delivery, invoiced directly.
